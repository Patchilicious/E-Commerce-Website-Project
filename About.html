About Me

Background

I was born and raised in Mira Mesa, San Diego on August 18th, 2001. I graduated Mira Mesa High School in 2019, went to Miramar Community College until 2022, then transferred to San Diego State University where I currently am majoring in Management Information Systems and expected to graduate in 2026. Throughout my life, computers and technology have alway fascinated me, from playing video games to first learning how to code the Snake Game I have always had a love of learning about computers and building them.

Education & Skills

When I first went to Miramar Community College, I went in as a Computer Science major and graduated with an associates in Computer Science. However, once I reached SDSU, I realized that I greatly enjoyed the business side of things during my electives and thus, switched my major to MIS, or Management Information Systems. This allowed me to combine a budding field and a lifetime interest in computers. Throughout my years at college I learned many coding languages, such as Python, C, C++, HTML, SQL, and Java, combined with learning business applications such as SAP and Excel I have quickly accumulated a wide variety of skills that I believe many employers will benefit from. If you want to learn more, head over to the resume page.

Interests & Hobbies

Outside of academics, I enjoy video games, playing tabletop games like Warhammer 40K and Daggerheart, going to the gym and working out, camping, and engaging in many other creative projects. Reading Fantasy, Sci-Fi, and horror have become a new hobby for me, especially listening to audiobooks whilst I assemble and paint miniatures for Warhammer 40K.
